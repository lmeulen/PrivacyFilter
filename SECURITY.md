# Security policy

## Supported version

| Version | Supported |
| --- | --- |
| 2.x | Yes |
| < 2.0 | No |

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not open public issues for suspected vulnerabilities or privacy bypasses. Email `leo.vander.meulen@gmail.com` with reproduction steps, affected version, impact, and any suggested mitigation. Expect acknowledgement within 7 days.

## Privacy limitation

PrivacyFilter is heuristic PII reduction, not a guarantee of anonymization, legal compliance, or safe publication. False negatives and false positives can occur. Validate output for your language, dataset, threat model, and applicable privacy law before sharing it.
